# Artwork

These images are the Bongo Cat artwork used by `bongo-cat-mode` in the
mode-line: angled and flat cat frames in white and black variants, plus
solid and faint rail tiles for `bongo-cat-scroll-mode`.

The artwork is adapted from the "Coding Cat" design and redrawn for the
mode-line (laptop and headphones removed).

Each frame is provided twice:

- `.svg` - the editable source
- `.png` - the rendered frame shipped at run time
